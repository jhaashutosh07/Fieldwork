AI agent · evaluation · production reliability

An AI agent that cleans a messy sales CRM, and proves it got it right.

CRMs fill up with duplicates, old company names, stale headcounts and dead websites. Fieldwork resolves every row to the real company, fills in current facts with their source and date, decides who is worth selling to, and hands anything uncertain to a human instead of guessing. Every number here is scored against real Wikidata ground truth, on companies never used to tune the agent.

Clean your own CRM

Paste or upload a CSV (up to 50 rows). Duplicates are merged, old names and stale data fixed, every value gets its source and date, and anything doubtful is flagged for a human.

Columns: account_name (or name / company), optional website, hq_country, employee_count, last_updated.

Results

Held-out test split: 158 companies, 242 CRM rows that no fix was tuned on. Every change was driven by failures triaged on a separate dev split.
