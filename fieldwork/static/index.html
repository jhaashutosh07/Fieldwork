AI agent · evaluation · production reliability

An AI agent that cleans a messy sales CRM, and proves it got it right.

CRMs fill up with duplicates, old company names, stale headcounts and dead websites. Fieldwork resolves every row to the real company, fills in current facts with their source and date, decides who is worth selling to, and hands anything uncertain to a human instead of guessing. Every number on this page is scored against real ground truth from Wikidata, on companies that were never used to tune the agent.

  1. ResolveWebsite lookup first, then a registry of 3,598 listed companies
  2. Merge“adobe” and “ADBE” become one account
  3. ReconcileNewest dated value wins, with its source
  4. JudgeRules first; an LLM only for what rules can't settle
  5. GuardrailNot sure? Send it to a human, never guess
